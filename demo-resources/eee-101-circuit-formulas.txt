DEMO RESOURCE - Circuit Analysis Formula Sheet

Ohm's law:
V = I R

Power:
P = V I = I^2 R = V^2 / R

Series resistors:
R_total = R1 + R2 + ...

Parallel resistors:
1 / R_total = 1 / R1 + 1 / R2 + ...

Kirchhoff's Current Law:
The algebraic sum of currents entering a node is zero.

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law:
The algebraic sum of voltage rises and drops around a closed loop is zero.

Always check units and choose a consistent reference direction for current
and voltage. These are original demo notes, not official course material.
